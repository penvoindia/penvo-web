# Testimonial sample assets

Generated on 2026-10-09 with the built-in `image_gen` tool. These images depict fictional, anonymous sample identities for the testimonial layout. They do not represent verified Penvo clients or endorsements. The story image is a poster only; there is no testimonial video.

Final files live in the project under `public/testimonials/`. Generated originals remain in Codex's generated-images directory. The final assets were resized and converted with the project's existing Sharp dependency; no dependency was added.

All outputs were visually inspected after conversion. The story portrait preserves the subject's face and a darker lower third for an author overlay. The avatars keep centered faces for circular crops.
